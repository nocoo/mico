# Factual answer

For a 2n = 4 germ-line cell:

- Before division, DNA replication gives four chromosomes, each with two sister chromatids; chromosome number is 4 and chromatid number is 8.
- Meiosis I pairs homologous chromosomes, allows crossing over, and separates homologous chromosomes; after meiosis I, two cells each contain 2 chromosomes and 4 chromatids.
- Meiosis II separates sister chromatids; four daughter cells are produced, each with 2 chromosomes and 1 chromatid per chromosome.
- Independent assortment across a population of meiotic cells can generate 2² = 4 parental combinations for two chromosome pairs. One 2n = 4 meiotic cell has only one meiosis I orientation and, without crossover, produces two parental combinations represented by two copies each among its four gametes.
- Chromosome number is halved in meiosis I; chromatids are separated in meiosis II.

# Scoring: 10 points

- 2 points: begins with 2n = 4: two homologous pairs, with clear paternal/maternal colors and four chromosomes containing sister chromatids before reduction.
- 2 points: prophase I shows homologous pairing and a clearly recognizable crossing-over/X-shaped exchange.
- 2 points: meiosis I shows homologous chromosomes separated into two cells of n = 2; chromosomes are not incorrectly separated into single chromatids here.
- 2 points: meiosis II shows sister chromatids separate and yields four haploid cells.
- 1 point: each final gamete has exactly 2 chromosomes, and all four gametes match the single meiosis I orientation depicted, with each parental combination shown consistently.
- 1 point: legend distinguishes chromosomes, sister chromatids, homologous pairs, and/or n/2n counts consistently.

# Accepted equivalents

- One or more recombination crosses may be shown by exchanged colored segments.
- Independent assortment across separate meiotic outcomes may be represented by a second alignment, but not as four different gametes from one single alignment. Exact gamete sequences are accepted if genetically consistent with the depicted pair separation.
- Pre-meiosis S phase may be combined with the initial 2n = 4 diagram rather than a separate stage.
- A chromosome bar, X-shaped pair, or duplicated-line symbol is accepted if its chromatid count is unambiguous.
