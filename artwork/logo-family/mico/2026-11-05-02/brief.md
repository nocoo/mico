# Mico logo study — Flare

Mico is a browser-based shared canvas where people and AI draw together. The owner supplied a whiteboard-character reference and requested its overall design and material quality, without cast shadows, for the favicon, assistant button and README.

Reference: `references/source.jpg`, copied without modification from the owner's Downloads image. Native size: 960 × 960; SHA-256: `9330e8ae798b01c4ec71d3c6f305bca5cfe00499a277ee44c33dcb94f34fde88`.

The first Sunburst result was superseded by the owner's instruction: “你参考这个风格，用gpt 2.5 image flare重新生成，再裁剪使用。” This instruction requests Flare generation and subsequent cropping and use in Mico. It does not assert that the owner reviewed the new image bytes.

The owner's explicit reference overrides the default animal-family direction. Keep the whiteboard silhouette, soft molded material, blush and blue details, and clean outer edges. A white matte enables a separate transparent export.
